# Rejected Alternative Model Proposal

## Purpose

Define a future research model for rejected ideas without changing Core.

## Lifecycle

Proposal
→ Rejected Decision
→ Reason
→ Evidence
→ History
→ Searchable archive

## Rules

- Rejected alternatives are preserved.
- They are excluded from active knowledge.
- They are excluded from Canon unless separately approved.
- Automatic resurrection is forbidden.

Status: Research proposal.
